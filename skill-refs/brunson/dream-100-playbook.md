# Dream 100 Playbook

The Dream 100 is a distribution strategy, not a traffic tactic. The logic: your buyers already congregate somewhere. Find the 100 people/places that have already aggregated your audience, then infiltrate — either by earning access (work your way in) or buying access (buy your way in).

Source: Traffic Secrets, Secrets #3 and #4.

---

## The core insight

Every market has people who have already done the hard work of gathering your dream customers into audiences. Your job is not to find customers from scratch. Your job is to get in front of the audiences that already exist.

How to build your Dream 100:
- Search your topic on podcasts, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, blogs
- Look for who publishes content your dream customers consume
- Find the Facebook groups they're in, the email lists they're subscribed to
- List them all — that's your Dream 100

---

## The two moves

**Work your way in (earned traffic — organic):**
Earn access to Dream 100 audiences without paying for ads.
- Guest on their podcast
- Co-author content / collab post
- Speak at their event
- Comment meaningfully in their community
- Provide value first (answer questions, share their content, refer clients)
- Interview them on your own show — then they promote the episode to their audience

Timeline: 3-6 months to build relationships. Higher trust, lower CAC.

**Buy your way in (controlled traffic — paid):**
Run ads targeted at Dream 100 audiences.
- Facebook/Instagram ads targeted at followers of specific accounts
- YouTube pre-roll on specific channels
- Newsletter sponsorships
- Podcast ads

Timeline: immediate traffic, higher CAC, needs a break-even funnel to work.

**The funnel-hacking principle for Dream 100:** before approaching anyone, become a real member of their world. Buy their products. Comment on their content. Share their stuff. Build a real relationship before asking for anything. The "good ol' boys club" unlocks once one insider vouches for you.

**Default for Pedro:** Start with work-your-way-in for PT SMB and personal brand. Layer paid when the MIFGE is break-even proven.

## The 5 congregation categories

For any offer, map the Dream 100 across these five categories:

| Category | What to look for | Example signals |
|---|---|---|
| Podcasts | Shows whose audience matches your buyer profile | Topics, guest list, listener demographics |
| YouTube channels | Creators in adjacent or complementary spaces | Subscriber count, engagement, topic overlap |
| Newsletters | Curated email lists with relevant audiences | Beehiiv/Substack in your niche |
| Communities | Facebook groups, Slack/Discord, forums, LinkedIn groups | Active members, topic relevance |
| Influencers / experts | Individual thought leaders your buyers follow | LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter |

## Dream 100 output format (what the skill produces)

When the Dream 100 pillar is invoked, produce:

1. **Offer + buyer summary** (confirm understanding before listing)
2. **5 congregation categories** with named examples (use training data; flag where Pedro needs to research)
3. **Top 10 highest-leverage targets** ranked by: audience size × relevance × access difficulty (lower = better)
4. **Infiltration plan per top 3:** work-your-way-in steps OR buy-your-way-in ad approach
5. **Handoff flag:** "For outbound copy to these targets → `/cold-email`"

## What Dream 100 is NOT

- It is not a cold outreach list (that is `cold-email`)
- It is not an ads targeting strategy (that is `paid-ads`)
- It is not SEO or content distribution (that is `content-strategy`)

Dream 100 is the *relational* layer. It identifies where to go and how to get in. The other skills handle the executional layer once access is established.
