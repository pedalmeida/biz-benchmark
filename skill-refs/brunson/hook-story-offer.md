# Hook / Story / Offer

The universal creative format. Applied at every layer of the funnel — not just at funnel-design level.

Source: Traffic Secrets, throughout. The framework applies to every piece of content and every page.

---

## The three parts

**Hook:** stops the scroll / opens the pattern interrupt. Gets attention. One sentence or image. Its only job is to make people stop and pay attention. Without the hook, nothing else matters — no one sees it.

Hook formats that work:
- Question: "Quantas chamadas perde a sua clínica por dia?"
- Curiosity gap: "The weird thing we did to double email open rates"
- Result + timeframe: "How I got 100 new patients in 90 days without extra staff"
- How To + Without: "How to [desired result] without [feared consequence]"

Bad hook: "Learn how to grow your business with funnels."
Good hook: "We built a €47 front-end that paid for every ad we ever ran. Here's the funnel."

**Story:** builds belief and connection. Delivers the epiphany before the offer. After the hook gets attention, the story creates desire.
- Formula: Backstory → Struggle → Discovery → Result → Bridge to offer
- Short version (ads, emails): 2-4 sentences
- Long version (VSL, webinar): full Epiphany Bridge (→ `references/story-stack.md`)
- Rule: people make decisions emotionally, justify logically. The story handles emotion.

**Offer:** makes the ask. Specific, tangible, one action.
- Formula: Here's what you get. Here's what it costs. Here's what to do now.
- Never bury the offer. State it clearly after the story lands.
- One CTA per page. Multiple CTAs split attention and reduce conversion.

---

## Applied at every funnel layer

| Layer | Hook | Story | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ad (social/search) | Scroll-stopping image or first line | 1-3 sentence micro-story | CTA to landing page |
| Landing page | Headline + subhead | VSL or bullet proof | Opt-in or buy button |
| Email (subject line) | Subject line = hook | Email body = story | CTA = offer |
| VSL / webinar | Opening hook (first 60 seconds) | Epiphany Bridge + three secrets | Stack slide + close |
| OTO page | "Wait — before you go" hook | Why this is the next logical step | One-click upsell |
| Bridge page | Pattern interrupt for cold traffic | Epiphany Bridge (short) | Redirect to offer |

---

## Traffic temperature changes how much story you need

- **Cold traffic:** hook must interrupt a stranger. Story must build trust from zero. Offer must be low-friction (free or cheap). Use a Bridge page to pre-frame before the offer page.
- **Warm traffic:** hook can reference shared context. Story can be shorter. Offer can be mid-ticket.
- **Hot traffic (your list):** hook can be direct. Minimal story. Offer can be high-ticket. Get to the point.

## How the Funnel Architect uses this

Include HSO guidance in blueprints at the page level. For each page in the blueprint, note:
- What the hook needs to accomplish (attention-grabbing mechanism)
- Whether a story is needed and how long
- What the offer/CTA is

Do not write the actual copy. Flag: "Hook/Story/Offer structure for this page → `copywriting`."
