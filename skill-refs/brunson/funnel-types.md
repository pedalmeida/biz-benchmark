# Funnel Types

**Underlying taxonomy (DotCom Secrets):**
- Frontend (cold traffic, list/buyer acquisition): Two-Step Free-Plus-Shipping, Self-Liquidating Offer, Continuity
- Middle (warm traffic, mid-ticket): Perfect Webinar, Invisible Funnel Webinar, Product Launch
- Backend (hot traffic, high-ticket): High-Ticket Three-Step Application

The skill routes using 6 simplified surface types. Each maps to the underlying DotCom Secrets mechanics.

---

## Routing decision matrix

| Route type | Maps to (DotCom) | Traffic temp | Price range | Primary use case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opt-in | Free-Plus-Shipping / SLO | Cold | Free / €0-€97 front-end | List building, break-even MIFGE entry |
| Application | High-Ticket Three-Step | Warm / Hot | €1,000+ | High-ticket service, consulting, done-for-you |
| Webinar | Perfect Webinar / Invisible | Warm | €297-€2,000 | One-to-many selling, group programs, courses |
| Summit | Product Launch variant | Cold / Warm | Free (list growth goal) | Authority building, partnerships, list explosion |
| Bridge | Pre-frame (all funnels) | Cold | Any | Traffic pre-frame before an offer page |
| Video Series | Product Launch (4-video) | Cold / Warm | €97-€997 | Cold-to-warm nurture, content-led conversion |

**Break-even rule:** any cold-traffic funnel defaults to break-even framing — front-end MIFGE or SLO recovers ad cost on day 1. Flag this in every cold-traffic blueprint without exception.

## Opt-in Funnel (maps to: Two-Step Free-Plus-Shipping or SLO)

**When:** building a list from cold traffic; MIFGE as front-end; continuity or email follow-up as real revenue.

**Two-Step Free-Plus-Shipping page sequence:**
1. Step 1 page: Hook + MIFGE offer, collects shipping address or email only (email captured here for follow-up on non-completers)
2. Step 2 page: Billing info + optional Order Form Bump (~34% take rate)
3. OTO 1: Upsell using OTO Script (3x-5x the front-end price)
4. OTO 2 (optional): Downsell or cross-sell if OTO 1 declined
5. Thank-you / bridge page: sets expectations, introduces Attractive Character

**SLO (Self-Liquidating Offer) page sequence:**
1. Squeeze page: email opt-in only, free lead magnet as bait
2. SLO sales page: €27-€97 core offer using Star/Story/Solution script + Order Form Bump
3. OTO 1: upsell using OTO Script
4. Thank-you page: next step + email sequence start

**Key asset per page:**
- Step 1 / Squeeze: hook headline + MIFGE description + single CTA. Script: Who (who you are) → What (what it is) → Why (why they need it) → How (how to get it) → The Catch (explain it's free/cheap) → Urgency → Guarantee → Recap.
- OTO pages: 12-step OTO Script — confirm decision, why smart, 3x/2x promise, exclusivity, speed, the one thing, future pace, CTA, guarantee, value stack, scarcity, second CTA.
- Thank-you: next step instruction + what to expect in email

**Split test starting point:** Headline on squeeze/step-1 page — curiosity hook vs. outcome-led. Real data: specific story headline beat control by +58%. See `references/split-test-winners.md`.

## Application Funnel (maps to: High-Ticket Three-Step Application)

**When:** high-ticket service or consulting (€1,000+); need to qualify before selling; close happens on a call.

**Page sequence (3 steps from DotCom Secrets):**
1. Free Case Study page: video showing client results. "Apply Now" CTA. Creates desire. No price.
2. Application page: multi-question form. Two jobs — gives sales team prospect info AND gets prospect to articulate why they'd succeed (self-sell mechanism).
3. Homework page: (a) personal video explaining your passion for helping; (b) sets call expectations (48-72 hrs); (c) 2-3 homework videos — short training + emotional story + testimonials. Phone number for hyperactive buyers.

**Sales process:** Setter/Closer two-person model. Setter qualifies and secures four commitments. Closer magnifies pain and closes. Handoff to `sales-enablement` for the full scripts.

**Key assets:**
- Case Study page: Hook (who gets results) + Story (client case) + CTA (apply)
- Application: qualifying questions only. No price on this page.
- Homework videos: emotional story + proof. Pre-call warm-up.

## Webinar Funnel (maps to: Perfect Webinar or Invisible Funnel Webinar)

**When:** one-to-many selling of a mid-ticket offer (€297-€2,000); traffic is warm or email list exists.

**Perfect Webinar page sequence:**
1. Registration page: Hook + promise + date/time
2. Confirmation page: show-up instructions + pre-webinar content
3. Live webinar: 60-90 min — Intro + One Big Domino + Three Secrets + Stack Close
4. Replay page: for no-shows (4 email sequences by behaviour: registered/no-show, watched all, watched partial, bought — each sequence stops when order is placed)
5. Order page: after webinar close
6. Post-purchase follow-up → `email-sequence`

**Invisible Funnel Webinar variant (try-before-you-buy, premium content):**
1. Registration page: Magic Bullet Script — collect CC upfront, not charged until after
2. Live webinar: 3-4 hours of high-value content
3. Soft close at end: transition to Application page for high-ticket
4. No charge if attendee emails before deadline — otherwise CC is billed

**Key asset:** Perfect Webinar Script (→ `references/story-stack.md`): Intro (5 min) → One Big Domino → 3 Secrets (epiphany bridges, ~50-60 min) → Stack Slide + 16 closes (~10-20 min).

**Tiny Challenge alternative:** 5x 1-hour live calls, one secret per call, sell on day 4-5. ~30% close on €500-€2,000. Better for cold audiences. Lower production overhead.

**Handoff:** Slide deck → `pedro-deck-builder`.

## Summit Funnel (maps to: Product Launch variant with partner traffic)

**When:** building authority and a large list fast; using other people's audiences via collaboration.

**Page sequence:**
1. Summit registration page (free access, high perceived value)
2. VIP upgrade page (recordings + bonuses, €47-€197)
3. Speaker opt-in thank-you pages (each speaker drives their list)
4. Post-summit offer page (back-end offer to warm list)

**Key dynamic:** speakers promote to their lists → you own the new subscribers. List growth is the primary KPI, not front-end revenue.

---

## Bridge Funnel (maps to: pre-frame layer, used before any funnel type)

**When:** cold traffic needs pre-framing before seeing an offer; affiliate or third-party offer; traffic temperature mismatch.

**Page sequence:**
1. Bridge page: story + pre-frame + recommendation using Epiphany Bridge structure
2. Redirect to offer page (your own or affiliate)

**Key asset:** Epiphany Bridge story on the bridge page — deliver the belief shift before the offer page sells. Warms cold traffic to warm before the offer page sees them.

**Use case for Pedro:** sending cold social or ad traffic to a high-ticket application or partner offer.

## Video Series Funnel (maps to: Product Launch 4-video structure)

**When:** cold-to-warm nurture needed; content-led conversion; building belief over multiple touchpoints.

**Page sequence (Product Launch 4-video structure from DotCom Secrets):**
1. Opt-in page: get access to the series (email capture)
2. Video 1 page — "Wow and How": Big Promise + kill myths + show others succeeding + hook to video 2
3. Video 2 page — "Transformational Education": walk through the process step by step + proof + hook to video 3
4. Video 3 page — "Ownership Experience": future-pace them doing it + hint at the offer + hook to video 4
5. Video 4 page — "The Offer": here's what I've got + how it works + what it'll do for you + order button

**Each video:** 10-20 minutes. Deliver real value. The Cart opens on Video 4.

**Key asset:** Product Launch Script (→ `references/story-stack.md`). Each video has a named script structure.

**Note:** Longer conversion path — use when offer is €197+ and audience is cold. Overkill for warm traffic.

## Seven Phases of a Funnel (DotCom Secrets — diagnostic framework)

Use this to audit any existing funnel or design a new one from scratch:

1. Determine traffic temperature (cold / warm / hot)
2. Set up the pre-frame bridge (prime visitor for what comes next; cold needs the longest bridge)
3. Qualify subscribers (opt-in page, get email)
4. Qualify buyers (separate buyers from non-buyers via small purchase or free-plus-shipping)
5. Identify hyperactive buyers (OTOs, order bumps, upsells for people ready to buy more now)
6. Age and ascend the relationship (email sequences, move them up the Value Ladder)
7. Change the selling environment (for high-ticket: move from webpage to phone, webinar, or live event)
