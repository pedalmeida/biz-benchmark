# The Value Equation — Applied to Consulting Offers

```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```

To increase perceived value: increase the numerator, decrease the denominator.
The most common mistake: founders obsess over Dream Outcome and ignore Perceived Likelihood.

---

## The Four Variables

**Dream Outcome**
What the buyer explicitly or secretly wants to be true after working with you.
Not what you deliver — what they *have* at the end.
- Weak: "Better marketing automation"
- Strong: "12 qualified leads per week without Pedro touching the CRM"

**Perceived Likelihood of Achievement**
The buyer's confidence that they will actually get the Dream Outcome — with you, specifically.
Increased by: case studies, guarantees, track record, specificity of your process, credentials.
Decreased by: vague promises, no proof, generic language.

**Time Delay**
How long until they experience the Dream Outcome.
Shorter = higher value. The faster the first result, the more they trust the rest.
Even a small "quick win" in week one restructures their confidence.

**Effort & Sacrifice**
What the buyer has to do or give up to get the result.
Includes: time, internal meetings, change management, learning curve, emotional energy.
Lower = higher value. The less you ask of them, the easier the yes.

## Worked Example 1 — AI Voice Receptionist for Dental Clinic

**Offer:** AI voice receptionist that handles inbound calls, books appointments, reduces no-shows.

| Variable | Weak Version | Strong Version |
|---|---|---|
| Dream Outcome | "Automate your front desk" | "Zero missed calls. Your front desk handles twice the volume without a second hire. You sleep." |
| Perceived Likelihood | "Our AI is trained on dental workflows" | "We deployed this in 4 clinics in Lisbon. Average: 23% more booked appointments in 60 days. We guarantee 15% or we refund week 3 entirely." |
| Time Delay | "Full deployment in 4–6 weeks" | "Live within 9 business days. First call handled before your next staff meeting." |
| Effort & Sacrifice | "Your team will need to train on the platform" | "We handle everything. Your team does a 45-minute onboarding call. That's it." |

**Value Equation score before/after:**
- Before: Dream Outcome = 3, Likelihood = 2, Time Delay = 4 weeks (bad), Effort = medium
- After: Dream Outcome = 5, Likelihood = 4, Time Delay = 9 days (good), Effort = minimal

## Worked Example 2 — AI-Powered Client Onboarding for Wellness Practitioners

**Offer:** Automated onboarding flow (forms, intake, scheduling, first session prep) for yoga studios and therapists.

**Problem:** Practitioners lose 3–5 hours/week on admin that could be automated. New clients drop off between booking and first session (average 22% no-show rate).

| Variable | Weak | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Dream Outcome | "Streamline your client onboarding" | "Your new client goes from booking to first session with zero back-and-forth from you. No more chasing intake forms at 11pm." |
| Perceived Likelihood | "Our tool integrates with Calendly" | "We've done this for 11 practitioners in Portugal. Average no-show rate dropped from 22% to 7% within 90 days." |
| Time Delay | "2-month implementation" | "Your first automated client onboarded within 14 days of signing. We build while you teach." |
| Effort & Sacrifice | "You'll need to set up the workflows" | "We configure everything. You review one draft and approve. That's your only job." |

## Application Checklist

Before shipping any offer, score each variable 1–5:

```
Dream Outcome:         _ / 5
Perceived Likelihood:  _ / 5
Time Delay:            _ / 5 (5 = very fast, 1 = very slow)
Effort & Sacrifice:    _ / 5 (5 = no effort, 1 = lots of effort)

Total score: __ / 20

If any variable is below 3: fix it before sending.
If Perceived Likelihood is below 4: add proof or strengthen the guarantee.
```
